      How to play

      Find the mystery Manhattan spot in 6 guesses. New puzzle every day at midnight NYC time.

      1. 1
        Read the clue. Then type a place name or tap the blank map to drop a pin (tap “Guess this spot” to lock it in).
      2. 2
        Each guess shows the distance (miles & blocks), a direction arrow and how close you are. Manhattan’s grid tilts ~29°, so we also say uptown, downtown or crosstown.
      3. 3
        Every miss unlocks a hint: more clues, the neighborhood, the first letter, then a search zone on the map.
      4. 4
        Win by naming the place or dropping a pin within ~75 m (about a block).
      < ¼ mi < 1 mi < 3 mi 3+ mi Got it!

      Daily puzzles mix iconic landmarks with local favorites — delis, dive bars, hidden parks and ghost stations. Want more? Switch to Practice for unlimited rounds.
